# What is CodeMie Connect?

CodeMie Connect is the desktop app for CodeMie CLI. It walks through sign-in and profile setup, installs, updates, and removes coding tools, and runs health checks (the same as `codemie doctor`) with a suggested fix for every problem.

The **what CodeMie ran** bar at the bottom of the window lists every command the app ran, so nothing happens out of sight. Tools are still started from a terminal; the app only sets them up.
