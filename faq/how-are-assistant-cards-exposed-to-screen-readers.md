# How are assistant and template cards exposed to screen readers in CodeMie?

Assistant and template cards are rendered as semantic lists. Screen readers announce each card collection as a list and report the number of items, which allows navigating between cards item by item.

This applies to assistant cards in the Marketplace (including related assistants) and on the Project Assistants page, and to template cards on the Templates page.
