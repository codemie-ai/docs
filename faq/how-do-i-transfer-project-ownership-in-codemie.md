# How do I transfer project ownership in CodeMie?

Open **Settings → Administration → Projects management**, click the **⋮ (Actions)** menu on the
project row, and select **Change ownership**. In the dialog that opens, search for the new owner
by name or email, select them from the results, and click **Confirm**. The projects list refreshes
automatically to show the updated owner.

The option is only visible to **Platform Admins** and **Project Admins** of the project.
Personal projects do not support ownership transfer and do not show this option.
