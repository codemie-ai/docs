# How do I turn member spend limit enforcement on or off for a project in CodeMie?

Member spend limit enforcement is controlled by the **Enforce member spend limits** toggle in the project's **Edit Project** dialog. The toggle is available when budget management is enabled.

1. Go to **Profile → Settings → Administration → Projects Management** and select the project.
2. Click **Edit Project**.
3. Switch **Enforce member spend limits** on or off and click **Save**.
4. Read the confirmation dialog and click **Turn on enforcement** or **Turn off enforcement**.

Turning enforcement **on** caps each member at their own limit (their share of the project budget, or their fixed limit if they have one), and members already over their limit are blocked right away. Turning it **off** lets members draw on the full project budget; the project budget cap still applies, spend is still tracked per member, and members with a fixed limit stay capped.

Clicking **Cancel** in the confirmation dialog saves nothing: the toggle returns to its stored value and **Edit Project** stays open with any other edits intact.
