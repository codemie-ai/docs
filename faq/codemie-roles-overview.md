# What roles does CodeMie have and what can each role do?

CodeMie has five role levels:

1. **Maintainer** — highest operational role; includes all Admin rights plus exclusive
   control over budget and billing. Assigned directly in CodeMie (not a Keycloak realm role).
2. **Admin** — platform-wide access; manages users, access assignments, and platform settings.
   Does not include Maintainer-only budget controls.
3. **Project Admin** — admin rights scoped to specific projects only; manages members and
   project-level access; can view project spending trends.
4. **Regular User** — accesses only assigned projects and knowledge sources; can view own
   usage analytics.
5. **External User** — similar to Regular User, but with stricter limits for external or guest scenarios.
