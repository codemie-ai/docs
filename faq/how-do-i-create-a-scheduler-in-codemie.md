# How do I create a scheduler in CodeMie?

Navigate to **Schedulers** in the left sidebar and click **+ Create**. Fill in the project, alias, cron expression, timezone, resource type (Assistant, Workflow, or Datasource), and resource ID, then click **Save**. The scheduler appears in the list immediately and begins firing according to the configured schedule if **Is Enabled** was toggled on.

For a full walkthrough of each field see [Creating a Scheduler](https://docs.codemie.ai/user-guide/schedulers/create-scheduler).
