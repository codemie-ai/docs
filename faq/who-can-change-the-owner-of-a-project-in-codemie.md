# Who can change the owner of a project in CodeMie?

Only **Platform Admins** (Super Admins) and **Project Admins** of a specific project can change
that project's ownership. Regular project members do not see the **Change ownership** option in
the actions menu.

Additionally, ownership transfer is not available for **personal projects** — the option is
hidden for them regardless of the user's role.
