# Can external users or non-owners share an assistant with specific users?

No. Only the assistant owner can grant or revoke per-user access, and only internal CodeMie users can be selected. External (non-organization) users do not appear in the search results.

Users who were granted access to an assistant can use it but cannot see the **Share** action or re-share the assistant. To make an assistant available outside the project, publish it to the Marketplace instead.
